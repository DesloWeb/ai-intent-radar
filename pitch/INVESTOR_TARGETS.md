## The Pitch to Investors

**One line:** "We built Bloomberg Terminal for commercial intent — we tell businesses where money is
about to move before the opportunity is publicly listed."

**The gap we fill:**
- ZoomInfo tells you who to call. We tell you *why right now*.
- LinkedIn tells you who got hired. We tell you what *that hiring means commercially*.
- Google Alerts shows you news. We score it, rank it, and tell you what to do next.

**Traction:**
- Live product deployed at https://ai-intent-radar.vercel.app
- Real-time signal ingestion from Hacker News (more sources in roadmap)
- AI pipeline scoring commercial intent with confidence and urgency ratings
- Provider matching engine connecting opportunities to businesses that can serve them

**Ask:** $150K–$300K pre-seed to hire one engineer and fund 12 months of growth.
